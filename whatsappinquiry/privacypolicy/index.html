Privacy Policy

How Michael Data Solutions handles the information you share when you message our WhatsApp inquiry assistant.

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

This policy covers the automated assistant Michael Data Solutions ("MDS", "we", "us") operates on WhatsApp to answer inquiries about our services, provide pricing information, and book consultation calls. It explains what we collect when you message us, how that information is used, who we share it with, and how you can ask us to delete it.

This policy applies only to conversations with our WhatsApp inquiry assistant. It does not cover other Michael Data Solutions services or websites, which may be governed by their own terms.

2. Information We Collect

When you message our WhatsApp number, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number.
  • The content of the messages you send us.
  • Your name and company, if you share them or they come up naturally in conversation.
  • Which of our services you're interested in (Automations, Software, or Data Solutions), based on what you tell us.
  • If you ask to book a consultation call: the date and time you're booked for.

We only collect what you actually tell us — there is no separate intake form, and we never ask for anything beyond what's needed to answer your inquiry and, if relevant, schedule a call.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to you. Your message is used to generate a relevant reply about our services, pricing, or availability.
  • To keep a record of the inquiry. We save your name, company, area of interest, and message count as a lead record, so our team can follow up and doesn't lose track of a conversation.
  • To book consultation calls. If you ask to schedule a call, we create a calendar event containing your name, phone number, company, and stated area of interest, so our team is prepared for the call.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

4. Third-Party Services We Rely On

To operate this assistant, your information passes through a small number of service providers, strictly to deliver the functionality described above:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform. Messages are sent and received through WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta and governed by WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API). Your message content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft a reply and identify relevant details (like your stated area of interest). Anthropic processes this data on our behalf to provide that service.
  • Google Workspace. Lead records and consultation call events are stored in Google Sheets and Google Calendar, under our Google Workspace account.

None of these providers are permitted to use your information for their own purposes beyond delivering the service to us.

5. Data Retention

  • Active conversation context is held temporarily (for about one hour after your last message) so the assistant can follow the thread of a conversation, then automatically expires.
  • Your lead record (name, company, area of interest, and meeting details, if any) is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until it's no longer needed for our business relationship with you.

6. Your Choices & Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@michaeldatasolutions.com. There's no opt-out command needed to stop the conversation — this is a one-to-one inquiry channel, not a broadcast or marketing list, so simply not replying ends the interaction.

7. Children's Privacy

This assistant is intended for business inquiries from adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this channel.

8. Data Security

Your information is stored within Google Workspace and processed via Anthropic's API, both of which maintain their own security standards. We do not maintain a separate public-facing database of this data, and access is limited to the Michael Data Solutions team.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the assistant's functionality changes. The "Effective Date" above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled? Reach us at info@michaeldatasolutions.com.
